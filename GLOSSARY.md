# Glossary

The terms the shelf uses, what each means here, and how each language renders it. It keeps
the seven languages consistent from piece to piece; follow it when a piece is added or
translated, and add to it when a piece brings a new term.

The English follows Ben Dykes's *Traditional Natal Astrology Course*, which the pieces teach
from and gloss (`<span class="gloss" lang="en">`). Where the course has its own word
(*native*, *Lot*, *meeting* and *prevention*, *equation*, *pole* for the axis), the pieces
keep it.

Each entry gives the headword, a short definition, and the rendering in Norsk bokmål (nb),
Español (es), Français (fr), Deutsch (de), 简体中文 (zh) and 日本語 (ja). *Proposed* marks a
rendering not yet used on the shelf. *Not* lists forms to avoid; those set as `code` are
wrong in any context, and [`tests/glossary/`](tests/glossary/) fails if one appears. Key captions fixed in the
HTML (the Takht's **RAMC**, **LST**, **sign**, **sto**, **accel**) stay as they are in every
language.

## A

- **Absolute longitude.** Longitude as 0–360°, as opposed to sign and degree (12° ♐ 30′).
  **nb** absolutt lengde / grad innenfor tegnet · **es** longitud absoluta / grado dentro del signo · **fr** longitude absolue / degré dans le signe · **de** absolute Länge / Grad im Zeichen · **zh** 绝对黄经 / 星座内度数 · **ja** 絶対黄経／サイン内の度数
- **Acceleration.** The correction of 9.86 seconds per hour of UT that turns elapsed clock time into sidereal time.
  **nb** akselerasjon (9,86 sekunder per time) · **es** aceleración (9,86 s por hora) · **fr** accélération (9,86 secondes par heure) · **de** Beschleunigung (9,86 Sekunden pro Stunde) · **zh** 加速（每小时 9.86 秒） · **ja** 加速（1 時間あたり 9.86 秒）
  *Not:* zh: `加速度`
- **Alchabitius Semi-Arc.** The house system of the lesson's tables of houses: the Ascendant's semi-arcs trisected on the equator and carried to the ecliptic. Also spelled *Alcabitius*. Kept as the tables print it.
  **nb** Alchabitius Semi-Arc *(proposed)* · **es** Alchabitius Semi-Arc *(proposed)* · **fr** Alchabitius Semi-Arc *(proposed)* · **de** Alchabitius Semi-Arc *(proposed)* · **zh** Alchabitius 半弧宫制 *(proposed)* · **ja** アルカビティウス半弧法 *(proposed)*
- **Almucantarath.** A small circle of equal altitude, parallel to the horizon. The course uses the word as a synonym of altitude.
  **nb** almukantarat · **es** almicantarat (pl. almicantarats) · **fr** almicantarat · **de** Almukantarat (Pl. Almukantarate) · **zh** 等高圈 · **ja** 等高度圏
  *Not:* es: `almucantarath`; fr: `almucantarat`; zh: `地平纬圈`
- **Alternative members, alternative member sets.** One of several members, or one whole set out of several; never all of them together.
  **nb** Alternative medlemmer / Alternative medlemssett *(proposed)* · **es** Miembros alternativos / Conjuntos alternativos de miembros *(proposed)* · **fr** Membres alternatifs / Ensembles alternatifs de membres *(proposed)* · **de** Alternative Mitglieder / Alternative Mitgliedergruppen *(proposed)* · **zh** 备选成员 / 备选成员集合 *(proposed)* · **ja** 構成天体の別候補／構成天体の組の別候補 *(proposed)*
- **Altitude.** Height of a point above the horizon, 0° at the horizon to 90° at the zenith, negative below. *See* Almucantarath, Azimuth.
  **nb** høyde · **es** altura · **fr** hauteur (Haut.) · **de** Höhe · **zh** 高度 · **ja** 高度
  *Not:* es: `elevación`, `altitud`; fr: `altitude`; zh: `仰角`
- **Anomaly.** An inequality in a planet's motion: the zodiacal anomaly (from the eccentric) and the solar anomaly (from the epicycle).
  **nb** anomali (zodiakal anomali; retningsanomali) · **es** anomalía (zodiacal y direccional) · **fr** anomalie (zodiacale ; directionnelle) · **de** Anomalie im Tierkreis / Richtungsanomalie · **zh** 不等（黄道不等；方向上的不等） · **ja** 不等（獣帯上の不等） *(proposed)*
- **Apogee, perigee.** The farthest and nearest points of the deferent from the earth.
  **nb** apogeum / perigeum · **es** apogeo / perigeo · **fr** apogée / périgée · **de** Apogäum / Perigäum · **zh** 远地点 / 近地点 · **ja** 遠地点／近地点
- **Apsidal line.** The line through apogee, earth and perigee.
  **nb** apsidelinje · **es** línea de los ápsides · **fr** ligne des apsides · **de** Apsidenlinie · **zh** 拱线 · **ja** アプシス線
  *Not:* es: `línea apsidal`
- **Ascendant (ASC).** The degree of the ecliptic rising on the eastern horizon; in whole signs, the rising sign.
  **nb** ascendant (AC) · **es** Ascendente (ASC) · **fr** Ascendant (ASC) · **de** Aszendent (AC) · **zh** 上升点（ASC） · **ja** アセンダント（ASC）
  *Not:* nb: ASC as nb abbreviation (only as English gloss); es: `ascendente` (minúscula); de: ASC außer in Glossen; zh: `上升星座`
- **Atlas.** The book of places with their latitude and longitude; one of the three books beside the Takht.
  **nb** atlas · **es** atlas · **fr** atlas · **de** Atlas · **zh** 地图集 · **ja** 地図帳
  *Not:* ja: `アトラス`
- **Axial degrees, axes.** The Ascendant, Midheaven, Descendant and IC together.
  **nb** aksegrader / aksene · **es** grados axiales · **fr** degrés axiaux · **de** Achsgrade · **zh** 轴点度数（ASC、MC、DESC、IC） · **ja** 軸の度数
- **Axis of the poles.** The line through the two celestial poles, about which the sky turns. The course calls it the **pole**, and the pieces gloss it so; do not "correct" the gloss to *axis*.
  **nb** polakse (glossed «pole») · **es** eje de los polos (glosado «pole»); capa: «Eje polar» · **fr** axe des pôles · **de** Polachse (Gloss „pole“) · **zh** 天极轴（gloss “pole”） · **ja** 天の極軸（pole）
  *Not:* zh: 极轴
- **Azimuth.** Direction round the horizon, measured from north through east. With altitude it fixes a point in the horizon frame.
  **nb** asimut (Asim) · **es** acimut · **fr** azimut (Azim) · **de** das Azimut · **zh** 方位角（短标签作“方位”） · **ja** 方位角
  *Not:* es: `azimut`; de: `der Azimut`

## C

- **Carry, borrow.** Moving a full sixty into the next column, or taking one from it as sixty.
  **nb** mente / lån («ha 1 i mente», «låne 1 som 60») · **es** acarreo / préstamo (llevar; pedir prestado) · **fr** retenue / emprunt (reporter, emprunter) · **de** Übertrag, übertragen / Borgen, borgen · **zh** 进位 / 借位（满六十进一，借一当六十） · **ja** 繰り上がり／繰り下がり（60 として借りる）
- **Celestial equator.** The earth's equator projected onto the sky: a great circle 90° from both poles, cutting the horizon due east and west. *See* Declination, Right ascension.
  **nb** himmelekvator (kort: ekvator) · **es** ecuador celeste · **fr** équateur céleste · **de** Himmelsäquator (kurz: Äquator) · **zh** 天赤道（上下文明确时作“赤道”） · **ja** 天の赤道（略して赤道）
- **Celestial pole.** Either end of the axis of the poles. *The altitude of the pole is the latitude of the chart.*
  **nb** himmelpol (nordre/søndre himmelpol); polhøyde = breddegrad · **es** polo celeste (polo norte / polo sur) · **fr** pôle céleste (pôle nord / pôle sud) · **de** Himmelspol (Nordpol / Südpol); Polhöhe = geographische Breite · **zh** 天极（北天极 / 南天极） · **ja** 天の極（天の北極／天の南極）
- **Celestial sphere.** The sky pictured as a sphere turning about the earth; the stage of the three reference frames.
  **nb** himmelkule · **es** esfera celeste · **fr** sphère céleste · **de** Himmelssphäre (kurz: Sphäre) · **zh** 天球 · **ja** 天球
- **Certified, unavailable, none.** A certified computation; a result that cannot be given; an available result with no members. They are never interchanged.
  **nb** Sertifisert / Utilgjengelig / Ingen *(proposed)* · **es** Certificado / No disponible / Ningún elemento *(proposed)* · **fr** Certifié / Indisponible / Aucun élément *(proposed)* · **de** Zertifiziert / Nicht verfügbar / Keine *(proposed)* · **zh** 已认证 / 不可用 / 无 *(proposed)* · **ja** 検証済み／利用不可／なし *(proposed)*
- **Change sign.** Negate a value (±). Not a zodiacal sign.
  **nb** bytt fortegn (±) · **es** cambiar el signo (±) · **fr** changer le signe (±) · **de** Vorzeichen wechseln (±) · **zh** 改变正负号（±） · **ja** 符号を反転（±）
  *Not:* zh: `改变符号`
- **Chart.** The figure of the sky for a moment and place; *casting* (erecting) a chart is the Takht's purpose.
  **nb** horoskop (sette opp et horoskop) · **es** carta (levantar una carta) · **fr** thème (dresser un thème à la main) · **de** Horoskop (ein Horoskop stellen) · **zh** 星盘（起盘） · **ja** チャート（チャートを立てる）
  *Not:* es: `carta astronómica`, `horóscopo`; fr: `carte`, `horoscope`; ja: `ホロスコープ`
- **Chart wheel.** The drawn circle of a chart, with the zodiac round the rim and the axes across it.
  **nb** horoskophjul · **es** rueda de la carta · **fr** roue du thème · **de** Horoskoprad · **zh** 星盘圆图（行文中亦作“星盘”） · **ja** チャートの円
- **Circle of longitude.** The great circle through the poles of the ecliptic and a star. Not an hour circle.
  **nb** lengdesirkel *(proposed)* · **es** círculo de longitud *(proposed)* · **fr** cercle de longitude *(proposed)* · **de** Längenkreis *(proposed)* · **zh** 黄经圈 *(proposed)* · **ja** 黄経の大円 *(proposed)*
- **Clock time.** The time the clock showed at the birth, daylight saving included; clock time − UTC offset = UT.
  **nb** klokkeslett *(proposed)* · **es** hora oficial *(proposed)* · **fr** heure légale *(proposed)* · **de** Uhrzeit *(proposed)* · **zh** 钟表时间 *(proposed)* · **ja** 時計の時刻 *(proposed)*
  *Not:* de: Ortszeit (that is the local time of a meridian)
- **Co-anti-culminating degree.** The ecliptic degree crossing the lower meridian with the object.
  **nb** Grad som antikulminerer samtidig *(proposed)* · **es** Grado que anticulmina al mismo tiempo *(proposed)* · **fr** Degré qui anticulmine simultanément *(proposed)* · **de** Gleichzeitig antikulminierender Grad *(proposed)* · **zh** 同过下子午圈度数 *(proposed)* · **ja** 同時に北中する度数 *(proposed)*
- **Co-culminating degree.** The ecliptic degree crossing the upper meridian with the object.
  **nb** Grad som kulminerer samtidig *(proposed)* · **es** Grado que culmina al mismo tiempo *(proposed)* · **fr** Degré qui culmine simultanément *(proposed)* · **de** Gleichzeitig kulminierender Grad *(proposed)* · **zh** 同上中天度数 *(proposed)* · **ja** 同時に南中する度数 *(proposed)*
- **Co-rising degree.** The ecliptic degree rising when the object rises; not the object's ecliptic longitude.
  **nb** Grad som stiger samtidig *(proposed)* · **es** Grado que asciende al mismo tiempo *(proposed)* · **fr** Degré qui se lève simultanément *(proposed)* · **de** Gleichzeitig aufgehender Grad *(proposed)* · **zh** 同升度数 *(proposed)* · **ja** 同時に昇る度数 *(proposed)*

  **nb** Grad som går ned samtidig *(proposed)* · **es** Grado que se pone al mismo tiempo *(proposed)* · **fr** Degré qui se couche simultanément *(proposed)* · **de** Gleichzeitig untergehender Grad *(proposed)* · **zh** 同落度数 *(proposed)* · **ja** 同時に沈む度数 *(proposed)*
- **Column, row, box.** In a table of houses, columns run by sidereal time (4′ apart) and rows by latitude (1° apart); the four cells round the native form the box.
  **nb** kolonne (ST) / rad (breddegrad) / rute · **es** columna (de TS) / fila (de latitud) / recuadro; casilla = celda · **fr** colonne (TS) / ligne (latitude) / case · **de** Spalte (Sternzeit) / Zeile (Breite) / Kasten · **zh** 列（恒星时）/ 行（纬度）/ 方框；表格单元 · **ja** 列（恒星時）／行（緯度）／枠
  *Not:* zh: 表格（指单元格时）
- **Concentric.** Sharing the earth's centre.
  **nb** konsentrisk · **es** concéntrico · **fr** concentrique · **de** konzentrisch · **zh** 同心（同心圆） · **ja** 同心円（同心の）
- **Constellation figures.** The traditional figures as drawn; distinct from the zodiacal signs.
  **nb** Stjernebildefigurer *(proposed)* · **es** Figuras de las constelaciones *(proposed)* · **fr** Figures des constellations *(proposed)* · **de** Sternbildfiguren *(proposed)* · **zh** 星座形象 *(proposed)* · **ja** 星座の姿 *(proposed)*
- **Culminate, anti-culminate.** To cross the upper meridian (culminate) or the lower meridian (anti-culminate). The course (Lesson 4) puts it from a northern observer: stars "culminate in the south" and "anti-culminate in the north"; ja follows it with 南中／北中.
  **nb** kulminere / antikulminere · **es** culminar / anticulminar · **fr** culmination / anticulmination (culminer) · **de** kulminieren / Kulmination; Antikulmination (oberer/unterer Meridian) · **zh** 上中天 / 经过下子午圈 · **ja** 南中／北中（上の／下の子午圏を通過）
  *Not:* fr: `passage au méridien`
- **Curated list.** The default set of named stars and objects, labelled and listed; every star is still drawn as a point.
  **nb** Utvalgt liste *(proposed)* · **es** Lista selecta *(proposed)* · **fr** Liste choisie *(proposed)* · **de** Auswahlliste *(proposed)* · **zh** 精选列表 *(proposed)* · **ja** 厳選一覧 *(proposed)*
- **Cusp.** In quadrant houses, the degree where one house begins.
  **nb** husspiss *(proposed)* · **es** cúspide *(proposed)* · **fr** cuspide *(proposed)* · **de** Häuserspitze *(proposed)* · **zh** 宫头 *(proposed)* · **ja** カスプ *(proposed)*
- **Cutters.** A historical category of fixed stars, rendered literally.
  **nb** Skjærere *(proposed)* · **es** Cortadoras *(proposed)* · **fr** Tranchantes *(proposed)* · **de** Schneidende *(proposed)* · **zh** 切割者 *(proposed)* · **ja** 切るもの *(proposed)*

## D

- **Daylight saving time.** Summer time; subtract the shift in force at the birth, usually an hour, before converting a birth time to UT by the standard zone.
  **nb** sommertid · **es** horario de verano · **fr** heure d'été · **de** Sommerzeit · **zh** 夏令时 · **ja** 夏時間
  *Not:* fr: `heure avancée`
- **Declination (Dec).** Distance north or south of the celestial equator; the equator's counterpart of latitude.
  **nb** deklinasjon (Dekl) · **es** declinación (Dec) · **fr** déclinaison (Décl) · **de** Deklination (Dekl) · **zh** 赤纬（Dec） · **ja** 赤緯（Dec）
- **Deferent.** The large circle, off-centre from the earth, that carries a planet's epicycle.
  **nb** deferent · **es** deferente (círculo deferente) · **fr** déférent (cercle déférent) · **de** Deferent · **zh** 均轮 · **ja** 従円
- **Degrees, minutes, seconds (° ′ ″).** The sexagesimal units of arc; hours, minutes and seconds (h m s) are their counterpart in time.
  **nb** grader, minutter, sekunder (° ′ ″) · **es** grados, minutos, segundos (° ′ ″) · **fr** degrés, minutes, secondes (° ′ ″) · **de** Grad, Minuten, Sekunden (° ′ ″) · **zh** 度、分、秒（° ′ ″）；需区分时作角分、角秒 · **ja** 度・分・秒（° ′ ″）
- **Descendant (DESC).** The degree setting on the western horizon, opposite the Ascendant.
  **nb** descendant (DC) · **es** Descendente (DESC) · **fr** Descendant (DSC) · **de** Deszendent (DC) · **zh** 下降点（DESC） · **ja** ディセンダント（DESC）
  *Not:* fr: `DESC`
- **Difference.** The later table value minus the earlier one, before it is multiplied by the ratio. *See* Increment.
  **nb** differanse · **es** diferencia · **fr** différence · **de** Differenz · **zh** 差值 · **ja** 差
  *Not:* zh: `差额`
- **Direct calculation.** Working a value out by trigonometry instead of interpolating it from the table. Not *direct motion*.
  **nb** direkte beregning / direkte verdi · **es** cálculo directo / valor directo · **fr** calcul direct / valeur directe · **de** direkte Berechnung / direkter Wert · **zh** 直接计算（直接计算值） · **ja** 直接計算（直接計算の値）
  *Not:* nb: direkte bevegelse (that is direct motion); de: direktläufig; zh: `直接值`

## E

- **Earlier, later.** The two tabulated values either side of the native's: the earlier and later sidereal times (columns), MC and ASC.
  **nb** tidligere / senere · **es** anterior / posterior · **fr** précédent / suivant · **de** früher / später (frühere Sternzeit, späteres MC, späterer AC) · **zh** 较早的 / 较晚的 · **ja** 前の／後の
- **Eccentric, eccentricity.** A circle whose centre is not the earth; its eccentricity is how far off-centre it is.
  **nb** eksentrisk / eksentrisitet · **es** excéntrico / excentricidad · **fr** excentrique / excentricité · **de** Exzenter / exzentrisch / Exzentrizität · **zh** 偏心圆 / 偏心率 · **ja** 離心円／離心率
- **Ecliptic.** The Sun's yearly path, the great circle of zero ecliptic latitude, tilted to the equator by the obliquity. *See* Zodiac.
  **nb** ekliptikken · **es** eclíptica · **fr** écliptique (masc. : l'écliptique est incliné) · **de** Ekliptik · **zh** 黄道 · **ja** 黄道
- **Elongation.** A planet's angular distance from the Sun.
  **nb** elongasjon · **es** elongación · **fr** élongation · **de** Elongation · **zh** 距角 · **ja** 離角
- **Eminent.** A historical category of fixed stars: notable distinction. Not the same as *significant stars*.
  **nb** Fremstående *(proposed)* · **es** Eminentes *(proposed)* · **fr** Éminentes *(proposed)* · **de** Herausragend *(proposed)* · **zh** 显赫 *(proposed)* · **ja** 卓越 *(proposed)*
- **Ephemeris (pl. ephemerides).** A day-by-day table of planetary positions, usually for 0h UT.
  **nb** efemeride (pl. efemerider) · **es** efemérides (f. pl.; «unas efemérides») · **fr** éphémérides (fém. pl.) · **de** Ephemeride (Pl. Ephemeriden) · **zh** 星历表（简作“星历”） · **ja** 天文暦
  *Not:* fr: `un éphéméride`; ja: `エフェメリス`
- **Epicycle.** The small circle whose centre rides the deferent and on which the planet turns.
  **nb** episykel · **es** epiciclo · **fr** épicycle · **de** Epizykel · **zh** 本轮 · **ja** 周転円
- **Equant.** The point from which the epicycle's centre appears to move uniformly; set at twice the eccentricity. It lies almost exactly at the empty focus of the planet's ellipse.
  **nb** ekvant · **es** ecuante · **fr** équant · **de** Äquant · **zh** 匀速点 · **ja** エカント
  *Not:* es: `equante`
- **Equation.** The correction that turns a mean position into a true one, read from a table of equations. (In house theory the course also uses *equation* for quadrant division.)
  **nb** jevning / jevningstabell · **es** ecuación / tabla de ecuaciones · **fr** équation / table des équations · **de** Gleichung / Gleichungstafel · **zh** 均差 / 均差表 · **ja** 均差／均差表
- **Equinoctial point, 0° Aries.** Where the ecliptic crosses the equator northward; the origin of longitude and right ascension.
  **nb** jevndøgnspunktet / 0° Væren · **es** 0° de Aries (punto equinoccial) *(proposed)* · **fr** point équinoxial / 0° du Bélier *(proposed)* · **de** 0° Widder / Frühlingspunkt · **zh** 白羊座 0°（春分点） · **ja** 牡羊座 0°（春分点）
- **Eyes.** A historical category of fixed stars; neither a diagnosis nor a promise.
  **nb** Øyne *(proposed)* · **es** Ojos *(proposed)* · **fr** Yeux *(proposed)* · **de** Augen *(proposed)* · **zh** 眼睛 *(proposed)* · **ja** 目 *(proposed)*

## G

- **Great circle.** A circle on the sphere whose centre is the sphere's centre: horizon, meridian, prime vertical, equator, ecliptic.
  **nb** storsirkel · **es** círculo máximo · **fr** grand cercle · **de** Großkreis · **zh** 大圆 · **ja** 大円
  *Not:* es: `gran círculo`
- **Great conjunction, mean conjunction.** The meeting of Jupiter and Saturn about every twenty years. The *mean* conjunction is the one computed from mean motions; the Orrery places 2020's in September, three months before the true one.
  **nb** stor konjunksjon / middelkonjunksjon · **es** gran conjunción / conjunción media · **fr** grande conjonction / conjonction moyenne · **de** große Konjunktion / mittlere Konjunktion · **zh** 大合 / 平合（真合） · **ja** グレート・コンジャンクション／平均の合

  **nb** stjernetid i Greenwich (GST) · **es** tiempo sidéreo de Greenwich (TSG) · **fr** temps sidéral de Greenwich (TSG) · **de** Greenwicher Sternzeit (GST) · **zh** 格林尼治恒星时（GST） · **ja** グリニッジ恒星時（GST）
  *Not:* es: `GST`; fr: `GST`
