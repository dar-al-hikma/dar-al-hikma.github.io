# Glossary

The terms the shelf uses, what each means here, and how each language renders it. It keeps
the seven languages consistent from piece to piece; follow it when a piece is added or
translated, and add to it when a piece brings a new term.

The English follows Ben Dykes's *Traditional Natal Astrology Course*, which the pieces teach
from and gloss (`<span class="gloss" lang="en">`). Where the course has its own word
(*native*, *Lot*, *meeting* and *prevention*, *equation*, *pole* for the axis), the pieces
keep it.

Each entry gives the headword, a short definition, and the rendering in Norsk bokmål (nb),
Español (es), Français (fr), Deutsch (de), 简体中文 (zh) and 日本語 (ja). *Proposed* marks a
rendering not yet used on the shelf. *Not* lists forms to avoid; those set as `code` are
wrong in any context, and [`tests/glossary/`](tests/glossary/) fails if one appears. Key captions fixed in the
HTML (the Takht's **RAMC**, **LST**, **sign**, **sto**, **accel**) stay as they are in every
language.

## A

- **Absolute longitude.** Longitude as 0–360°, as opposed to sign and degree (12° ♐ 30′).
  **nb** absolutt lengde / grad innenfor tegnet · **es** longitud absoluta / grado dentro del signo · **fr** longitude absolue / degré dans le signe · **de** absolute Länge / Grad im Zeichen · **zh** 绝对黄经 / 星座内度数 · **ja** 絶対黄経／サイン内の度数
- **Acceleration.** The correction of 9.86 seconds per hour of UT that turns elapsed clock time into sidereal time.
  **nb** akselerasjon (9,86 sekunder per time) · **es** aceleración (9,86 s por hora) · **fr** accélération (9,86 secondes par heure) · **de** Beschleunigung (9,86 Sekunden pro Stunde) · **zh** 加速（每小时 9.86 秒） · **ja** 加速（1 時間あたり 9.86 秒）
  *Not:* zh: `加速度`
- **Almucantarath.** A small circle of equal altitude, parallel to the horizon. The course uses the word as a synonym of altitude.
  **nb** almukantarat · **es** almicantarat (pl. almicantarats) · **fr** almicantarat · **de** Almukantarat (Pl. Almukantarate) · **zh** 等高圈 · **ja** 等高度圏
  *Not:* es: `almucantarath`; fr: `almucantarat`; zh: `地平纬圈`
- **Altitude.** Height of a point above the horizon, 0° at the horizon to 90° at the zenith, negative below. *See* Almucantarath, Azimuth.
  **nb** høyde · **es** altura · **fr** hauteur (Haut.) · **de** Höhe · **zh** 高度 · **ja** 高度
  *Not:* es: `elevación`, `altitud`; fr: `altitude`; zh: `仰角`
- **Anomaly.** An inequality in a planet's motion: the zodiacal anomaly (from the eccentric) and the solar anomaly (from the epicycle).
  **nb** anomali (zodiakal anomali; retningsanomali) · **es** anomalía (zodiacal y direccional) · **fr** anomalie (zodiacale ; directionnelle) · **de** Anomalie im Tierkreis / Richtungsanomalie · **zh** 不等（黄道不等；方向上的不等） · **ja** 不等（獣帯上の不等） *(proposed)*
- **Apogee, perigee.** The farthest and nearest points of the deferent from the earth.
  **nb** apogeum / perigeum · **es** apogeo / perigeo · **fr** apogée / périgée · **de** Apogäum / Perigäum · **zh** 远地点 / 近地点 · **ja** 遠地点／近地点
- **Apsidal line.** The line through apogee, earth and perigee.
  **nb** apsidelinje · **es** línea de los ápsides · **fr** ligne des apsides · **de** Apsidenlinie · **zh** 拱线 · **ja** アプシス線
  *Not:* es: `línea apsidal`
- **Ascendant (ASC).** The degree of the ecliptic rising on the eastern horizon; in whole signs, the rising sign.
  **nb** ascendant (AC) · **es** Ascendente (ASC) · **fr** Ascendant (ASC) · **de** Aszendent (AC) · **zh** 上升点（ASC） · **ja** アセンダント（ASC）
  *Not:* nb: ASC as nb abbreviation (only as English gloss); es: `ascendente` (minúscula); de: ASC außer in Glossen; zh: `上升星座`
- **Axial degrees, axes.** The Ascendant, Midheaven, Descendant and IC together.
  **nb** aksegrader / aksene · **es** grados axiales · **fr** degrés axiaux · **de** Achsgrade · **zh** 轴点度数（ASC、MC、DESC、IC） · **ja** 軸の度数
- **Axis of the poles.** The line through the two celestial poles, about which the sky turns. The course calls it the **pole**, and the pieces gloss it so; do not "correct" the gloss to *axis*.
  **nb** polakse (glossed «pole») · **es** eje de los polos (glosado «pole»); capa: «Eje polar» · **fr** axe des pôles · **de** Polachse (Gloss „pole“) · **zh** 天极轴（gloss “pole”） · **ja** 天の極軸（pole）
  *Not:* zh: 极轴
- **Azimuth.** Direction round the horizon, measured from north through east. With altitude it fixes a point in the horizon frame.
  **nb** asimut (Asim) · **es** acimut · **fr** azimut (Azim) · **de** das Azimut · **zh** 方位角（短标签作“方位”） · **ja** 方位角
  *Not:* es: `azimut`; de: `der Azimut`

## C

- **Carry, borrow.** Moving a full sixty into the next column, or taking one from it as sixty.
  **nb** mente / lån («ha 1 i mente», «låne 1 som 60») · **es** acarreo / préstamo (llevar; pedir prestado) · **fr** retenue / emprunt (reporter, emprunter) · **de** Übertrag, übertragen / Borgen, borgen · **zh** 进位 / 借位（满六十进一，借一当六十） · **ja** 繰り上がり／繰り下がり（60 として借りる）
- **Celestial equator.** The earth's equator projected onto the sky: a great circle 90° from both poles, cutting the horizon due east and west. *See* Declination, Right ascension.
  **nb** himmelekvator (kort: ekvator) · **es** ecuador celeste · **fr** équateur céleste · **de** Himmelsäquator (kurz: Äquator) · **zh** 天赤道（上下文明确时作“赤道”） · **ja** 天の赤道（略して赤道）
- **Celestial pole.** Either end of the axis of the poles. *The altitude of the pole is the latitude of the chart.*
  **nb** himmelpol (nordre/søndre himmelpol); polhøyde = breddegrad · **es** polo celeste (polo norte / polo sur) · **fr** pôle céleste (pôle nord / pôle sud) · **de** Himmelspol (Nordpol / Südpol); Polhöhe = geographische Breite · **zh** 天极（北天极 / 南天极） · **ja** 天の極（天の北極／天の南極）
- **Celestial sphere.** The sky pictured as a sphere turning about the earth; the stage of the three reference frames.
  **nb** himmelkule · **es** esfera celeste · **fr** sphère céleste · **de** Himmelssphäre (kurz: Sphäre) · **zh** 天球 · **ja** 天球
- **Change sign.** Negate a value (±). Not a zodiacal sign.
  **nb** bytt fortegn (±) · **es** cambiar el signo (±) · **fr** changer le signe (±) · **de** Vorzeichen wechseln (±) · **zh** 改变正负号（±） · **ja** 符号を反転（±）
  *Not:* zh: `改变符号`
- **Chart.** The figure of the sky for a moment and place; *casting* (erecting) a chart is the Takht's purpose.
  **nb** horoskop (sette opp et horoskop) · **es** carta (levantar una carta) · **fr** thème (dresser un thème à la main) · **de** Horoskop (ein Horoskop stellen) · **zh** 星盘（起盘） · **ja** チャート（チャートを立てる）
  *Not:* es: `carta astronómica`, `horóscopo`; fr: `carte`, `horoscope`; ja: `ホロスコープ`
- **Chart wheel.** The drawn circle of a chart, with the zodiac round the rim and the axes across it.
  **nb** horoskophjul · **es** rueda de la carta · **fr** roue du thème · **de** Horoskoprad · **zh** 星盘圆图（行文中亦作“星盘”） · **ja** チャートの円
- **Column, row, box.** In a table of houses, columns run by sidereal time (4′ apart) and rows by latitude (1° apart); the four cells round the native form the box.
  **nb** kolonne (ST) / rad (breddegrad) / rute · **es** columna (de TS) / fila (de latitud) / recuadro; casilla = celda · **fr** colonne (TS) / ligne (latitude) / case · **de** Spalte (Sternzeit) / Zeile (Breite) / Kasten · **zh** 列（恒星时）/ 行（纬度）/ 方框；表格单元 · **ja** 列（恒星時）／行（緯度）／枠
  *Not:* zh: 表格（指单元格时）
- **Concentric.** Sharing the earth's centre.
  **nb** konsentrisk · **es** concéntrico · **fr** concentrique · **de** konzentrisch · **zh** 同心（同心圆） · **ja** 同心円（同心の）
- **Culminate, anti-culminate.** To cross the upper meridian (culminate) or the lower meridian (anti-culminate). The course (Lesson 4) puts it from a northern observer: stars "culminate in the south" and "anti-culminate in the north"; ja follows it with 南中／北中.
  **nb** kulminere / antikulminere · **es** culminar / anticulminar · **fr** culmination / anticulmination (culminer) · **de** kulminieren / Kulmination; Antikulmination (oberer/unterer Meridian) · **zh** 上中天 / 经过下子午圈 · **ja** 南中／北中（上の／下の子午圏を通過）
  *Not:* fr: `passage au méridien`
- **Cusp.** In quadrant houses, the degree where one house begins.
  **nb** husspiss *(proposed)* · **es** cúspide *(proposed)* · **fr** cuspide *(proposed)* · **de** Häuserspitze *(proposed)* · **zh** 宫头 *(proposed)* · **ja** カスプ *(proposed)*

## D

- **Daylight saving time.** Summer time; subtract an hour before converting a birth time to UT.
  **nb** sommertid · **es** horario de verano · **fr** heure d'été · **de** Sommerzeit · **zh** 夏令时 · **ja** 夏時間
  *Not:* fr: `heure avancée`
- **Declination (Dec).** Distance north or south of the celestial equator; the equator's counterpart of latitude.
  **nb** deklinasjon (Dekl) · **es** declinación (Dec) · **fr** déclinaison (Décl) · **de** Deklination (Dekl) · **zh** 赤纬（Dec） · **ja** 赤緯（Dec）
- **Deferent.** The large circle, off-centre from the earth, that carries a planet's epicycle.
  **nb** deferent · **es** deferente (círculo deferente) · **fr** déférent (cercle déférent) · **de** Deferent · **zh** 均轮 · **ja** 従円
- **Degrees, minutes, seconds (° ′ ″).** The sexagesimal units of arc; hours, minutes and seconds (h m s) are their counterpart in time.
  **nb** grader, minutter, sekunder (° ′ ″) · **es** grados, minutos, segundos (° ′ ″) · **fr** degrés, minutes, secondes (° ′ ″) · **de** Grad, Minuten, Sekunden (° ′ ″) · **zh** 度、分、秒（° ′ ″）；需区分时作角分、角秒 · **ja** 度・分・秒（° ′ ″）
- **Descendant (DESC).** The degree setting on the western horizon, opposite the Ascendant.
  **nb** descendant (DC) · **es** Descendente (DESC) · **fr** Descendant (DSC) · **de** Deszendent (DC) · **zh** 下降点（DESC） · **ja** ディセンダント（DESC）
  *Not:* fr: `DESC`
- **Direct calculation.** Working a value out by trigonometry instead of interpolating it from the table. Not *direct motion*.
  **nb** direkte beregning / direkte verdi · **es** cálculo directo / valor directo · **fr** calcul direct / valeur directe · **de** direkte Berechnung / direkter Wert · **zh** 直接计算（直接计算值） · **ja** 直接計算（直接計算の値）
  *Not:* nb: direkte bevegelse (that is direct motion); de: direktläufig; zh: `直接值`

## E

- **Eccentric, eccentricity.** A circle whose centre is not the earth; its eccentricity is how far off-centre it is.
  **nb** eksentrisk / eksentrisitet · **es** excéntrico / excentricidad · **fr** excentrique / excentricité · **de** Exzenter / exzentrisch / Exzentrizität · **zh** 偏心圆 / 偏心率 · **ja** 離心円／離心率
- **Ecliptic.** The Sun's yearly path, the great circle of zero ecliptic latitude, tilted to the equator by the obliquity. *See* Zodiac.
  **nb** ekliptikken · **es** eclíptica · **fr** écliptique (masc. : l'écliptique est incliné) · **de** Ekliptik · **zh** 黄道 · **ja** 黄道
- **Elongation.** A planet's angular distance from the Sun.
  **nb** elongasjon · **es** elongación · **fr** élongation · **de** Elongation · **zh** 距角 · **ja** 離角
- **Ephemeris (pl. ephemerides).** A day-by-day table of planetary positions, usually for 0h UT.
  **nb** efemeride (pl. efemerider) · **es** efemérides (f. pl.; «unas efemérides») · **fr** éphémérides (fém. pl.) · **de** Ephemeride (Pl. Ephemeriden) · **zh** 星历表（简作“星历”） · **ja** 天文暦
  *Not:* fr: `un éphéméride`; ja: `エフェメリス`
- **Epicycle.** The small circle whose centre rides the deferent and on which the planet turns.
  **nb** episykel · **es** epiciclo · **fr** épicycle · **de** Epizykel · **zh** 本轮 · **ja** 周転円
- **Equant.** The point from which the epicycle's centre appears to move uniformly; set at twice the eccentricity. It lies almost exactly at the empty focus of the planet's ellipse.
  **nb** ekvant · **es** ecuante · **fr** équant · **de** Äquant · **zh** 匀速点 · **ja** エカント
  *Not:* es: `equante`
- **Equation.** The correction that turns a mean position into a true one, read from a table of equations. (In house theory the course also uses *equation* for quadrant division.)
  **nb** jevning / jevningstabell · **es** ecuación / tabla de ecuaciones · **fr** équation / table des équations · **de** Gleichung / Gleichungstafel · **zh** 均差 / 均差表 · **ja** 均差／均差表
- **Equinoctial point, 0° Aries.** Where the ecliptic crosses the equator northward; the origin of longitude and right ascension.
  **nb** jevndøgnspunktet / 0° Væren · **es** 0° de Aries (punto equinoccial) *(proposed)* · **fr** point équinoxial / 0° du Bélier *(proposed)* · **de** 0° Widder / Frühlingspunkt · **zh** 白羊座 0°（春分点） · **ja** 牡羊座 0°（春分点）

## G

- **Great circle.** A circle on the sphere whose centre is the sphere's centre: horizon, meridian, prime vertical, equator, ecliptic.
  **nb** storsirkel · **es** círculo máximo · **fr** grand cercle · **de** Großkreis · **zh** 大圆 · **ja** 大円
  *Not:* es: `gran círculo`
- **Great conjunction, mean conjunction.** The meeting of Jupiter and Saturn about every twenty years. The *mean* conjunction is the one computed from mean motions; the Orrery places 2020's in September, three months before the true one.
  **nb** stor konjunksjon / middelkonjunksjon · **es** gran conjunción / conjunción media · **fr** grande conjonction / conjonction moyenne · **de** große Konjunktion / mittlere Konjunktion · **zh** 大合 / 平合（真合） · **ja** グレート・コンジャンクション／平均の合
- **Greenwich sidereal time (GST).** Sidereal time at Greenwich, read from the ephemeris for 0h UT.
  **nb** stjernetid i Greenwich (GST) · **es** tiempo sidéreo de Greenwich (TSG) · **fr** temps sidéral de Greenwich (TSG) · **de** Greenwicher Sternzeit (GST) · **zh** 格林尼治恒星时（GST） · **ja** グリニッジ恒星時（GST）
  *Not:* es: `GST`; fr: `GST`

  **nb** horisont · **es** horizonte · **fr** horizon · **de** Horizont · **zh** 地平圈（“地平之上/之下”，东地平/西地平） · **ja** 地平圏（地平）

## I

- **Imum Caeli (IC).** The degree on the lower meridian, opposite the MC. *See* Nadir.
  **nb** imum coeli (IC) · **es** Fondo del Cielo (IC) · **fr** Fond du Ciel (FC) · **de** Imum Coeli (IC) · **zh** 下中天（IC） · **ja** IC（イムム・コエリ）
  *Not:* nb: nadir (distinct point); fr: `IC`; zh: 天底
- **Increment.** The native's share of a difference: the ratio times the difference.
  **nb** tilvekst · **es** incremento (la parte de la nativa) · **fr** accroissement · **de** Anteil · **zh** 增量（命主分得的部分） · **ja** 増分（出生者の取り分）
  *Not:* fr: `incrément`; de: `Zuwachs`
- **Inferior, superior planet.** Mercury and Venus, which never stray far from the Sun; and Mars, Jupiter and Saturn, which can stand opposite it.
  **nb** indre / ytre planet · **es** planeta inferior / superior · **fr** planète inférieure / supérieure *(proposed)* · **de** unterer / oberer Planet · **zh** 内行星 / 外行星 *(proposed)* · **ja** 内惑星／外惑星 *(proposed)*
- **Interpolation.** Finding a value between two tabulated ones in proportion to the distance between them.
  **nb** interpolasjon / interpolere · **es** interpolación / interpolar · **fr** interpolation / interpoler · **de** Interpolation / interpolieren · **zh** 插值 · **ja** 補間（補間する）

## L

- **Latitude (ecliptic).** A planet's distance north or south of the ecliptic.
  **nb** bredde (ekliptisk bredde, Br) · **es** latitud (eclíptica) · **fr** latitude écliptique · **de** Breite (ekliptikale Breite) · **zh** 黄纬 · **ja** 黄緯

  **nb** mindre år · **es** años menores · **fr** petites années · **de** kleine Jahre · **zh** 小年 · **ja** 小年

## M

- **Mean, true.** *Mean* positions and motions are the uniform, idealised ones; *true* ones are what is seen. *See* Equation.
  **nb** middel / sann (middelsol, sann sol; middelbevegelse, middelposisjon) · **es** medio / verdadero (Sol medio, Sol verdadero) · **fr** moyen / vrai (Soleil moyen, Soleil vrai) · **de** mittel / wahr (mittlere Sonne, mittlerer Ort, wahrer Ort) · **zh** 平 / 真（平太阳、真太阳、平位置、真位置、平均运动） · **ja** 平均／真（平均太陽・真太陽、平均位置・真位置）
- **Meeting, prevention.** The course's words for the New Moon (meeting) and Full Moon (prevention) before a birth; hence *conjunctional* and *preventional* nativities.
  **nb** møte / preventio · **es** encuentro / prevención · **fr** rencontre / prévention · **de** Konjunktion (Neumond) / Prävention (Vollmond) · **zh** 合朔（标签作“朔”）/ 望；朔生 / 望生 · **ja** 朔／望（朔の出生／望の出生）
  *Not:* fr: nouvelle lune / pleine lune as terms
- **Meridian.** The north–south great circle through the zenith and the poles. Its upper half carries the Midheaven, its lower half the IC.
  **nb** meridian (øvre / nedre meridian) · **es** meridiano (superior / inferior) · **fr** méridien (supérieur / inférieur) · **de** Meridian (oberer / unterer) · **zh** 子午圈（上子午圈 / 下子午圈） · **ja** 子午圏（上の／下の子午圏）
  *Not:* zh: `子午线`
- **Midheaven (MC).** The degree of the ecliptic on the upper meridian; in whole signs, the tenth sign. In German it is neuter: *das MC*.
  **nb** midthimmel (MC) · **es** Medio Cielo (MC) · **fr** Milieu du Ciel (MC) · **de** das MC (Medium Coeli) · **zh** 中天（MC） · **ja** MC

## O

- **Oblique ascension, descension (OA, OD).** The right ascension of the Ascendant's degree as it rises (OA) or of the Descendant's as it sets (OD) at the given latitude. The course abbreviates OA ASC, OD DESC.
  **nb** skrå oppstigning / skrå nedstigning (OA / OD) · **es** ascensión oblicua (AO) / descensión oblicua (DO) · **fr** ascension oblique / descension oblique (AO / DO) · **de** schiefe Aufsteigung / schiefe Absteigung (AO / DO) · **zh** 斜升 / 斜降（OA / OD） · **ja** 斜上昇／斜下降（OA／OD）
  *Not:* es: OA, OD sin glosa; fr: OA / OD
- **Obliquity of the ecliptic (OE, ε).** The tilt of the ecliptic to the equator, about 23° 26′.
  **nb** ekliptikkens skjevhet (OE, ε); kort: skjevhet · **es** oblicuidad de la eclíptica (OE, ε) · **fr** obliquité de l'écliptique (OE, ε) · **de** Schiefe der Ekliptik (OE, ε); kurz: Schiefe · **zh** 黄赤交角（OE，ε） · **ja** 黄道傾斜角（OE, ε）
- **Opposite sign.** The sign 180° away; used when converting a northern table for a southern birth.
  **nb** motsatt tegn · **es** signo opuesto · **fr** signe opposé · **de** Gegenzeichen · **zh** 对宫（对宫星座） · **ja** 反対のサイン
  *Not:* zh: `对面的星座`

## P

- **Parallel of declination.** A small circle of constant declination, parallel to the equator. The Sphere's layer labels call these "declination circles"; render them as *parallels*, since "declination circle" elsewhere names the great circle through the poles.
  **nb** deklinasjonssirkel · **es** paralelo de declinación · **fr** parallèle de déclinaison (« cercle de déclinaison » dans les calques) · **de** Deklinationskreis · **zh** 赤纬圈 · **ja** 赤緯圏
  *Not:* nb: `timesirkel`; es: `círculo de declinación`, `círculo horario`; fr: `cercle horaire`; de: `Stundenkreis`; zh: `时圈`; ja: `時圏`
- **Pole of the ecliptic.** The point 90° from every point of the ecliptic. The Orrery looks down from it, so the zodiac lies flat as the rim.
  **nb** ekliptikkens pol · **es** polo de la eclíptica · **fr** pôle de l'écliptique · **de** Pol der Ekliptik · **zh** 黄极 · **ja** 黄道の極
- **Primary motion.** The daily east-to-west turning of the whole sky. *See* Secondary motion.
  **nb** primærbevegelsen (døgnbevegelsen) · **es** movimiento primario (o diurno) · **fr** mouvement primaire (diurne) · **de** Primärbewegung (tägliche Bewegung) · **zh** 第一运动（周日运动） · **ja** 第一運動（日周運動）
- **Prime vertical.** The great circle through the zenith and the east and west points of the horizon.
  **nb** førstevertikal · **es** primer vertical (m.) · **fr** premier vertical · **de** Erster Vertikal · **zh** 卯酉圈 · **ja** 卯酉圏

## Q

- **Quadrant.** One of the four quarters of the sky cut by the horizon and the meridian, or by the chart's axial degrees.
  **nb** kvadrant (østlig, sørlig, vestlig, nordlig) · **es** cuadrante (oriental, meridional, occidental, septentrional) · **fr** quadrant (oriental, méridional, occidental, septentrional) · **de** Quadrant · **zh** 象限（东/南/西/北象限） · **ja** 象限（東象限・南象限・西象限・北象限）
- **Quadrant houses.** Houses by dividing the quadrants between the axial degrees; they overlap the whole signs.
  **nb** kvadranthus (kvadrantbasert husdeling) · **es** casas por cuadrantes; domificación por cuadrantes · **fr** maisons par quadrants (domification par quadrants) · **de** Quadrantenhäuser (Häuserteilung nach Quadranten) · **zh** 象限分宫法 · **ja** 象限式のハウス分割

## R

- **RAMC.** Right ascension of the Midheaven: LST × 15, in degrees.
  **nb** RAMC (midthimmelens rektascensjon) · **es** ascensión recta del Medio Cielo (la ARMC) · **fr** ascension droite du Milieu du Ciel (ARMC) · **de** die RAMC · **zh** RAMC（中天赤经） · **ja** RAMC
  *Not:* es: RAMC in prose; fr: RAMC in prose; de: der RAMC
- **Ratio.** How far a value lies across an interval, as a fraction: the ST ratio, the latitude ratio, the birth-time ratio.
  **nb** forhold (ST-forhold, breddegradsforhold, fødselstidsforhold) · **es** proporción (de TS, de latitud, de la hora de nacimiento) · **fr** rapport (rapport de TS, rapport de latitude, rapport de l'heure de naissance) · **de** Faktor (Sternzeitfaktor, Breitenfaktor, Geburtszeitfaktor) · **zh** 比例（恒星时比例、纬度比例、出生时间比例） · **ja** 比（恒星時比・緯度比・出生時刻比）
  *Not:* es: razón, `relación`; fr: `proportion`; de: `Verhältnis`
- **Reckoner.** The arithmetic piece; also the old word for one who calculates.
  **nb** Regnemesteren · **es** El calculista · **fr** le Calculateur · **de** Der Rechenmeister · **zh** 《逢六十进一》 · **ja** 暦算（『暦算』）
  *Not:* fr: `la Calculatrice`
- **Reference frame.** One of the Sphere's three coordinate systems: the horizon frame (azimuth, altitude), the equatorial frame (right ascension, declination), the ecliptic frame (longitude, latitude).
  **nb** referanseramme (horisontrammen / ekvatorrammen / ekliptikkrammen) · **es** sistema de referencia (sistema horizontal / ecuatorial / eclíptico) · **fr** repère (horizontal / équatorial / écliptique) · **de** Bezugssystem (Horizontsystem / Äquatorsystem / Ekliptiksystem) · **zh** 坐标系（地平 / 赤道 / 黄道坐标系） · **ja** 座標系（地平座標系／赤道座標系／黄道座標系）
  *Not:* es: `marco`; fr: `référentiel`, `cadre`
- **Register, store, recall.** The Takht's memory: store a value in a register, recall it later.
  **nb** variabel (lagre / hente fram) · **es** variable / asignar / recuperar · **fr** variable / stocker / rappeler · **de** Variable / speichern / abrufen · **zh** 变量（存储 / 调用） · **ja** メモリー（変数メモリー）／登録／呼び出し
  *Not:* fr: `registre`, `mémoire`; zh: `寄存器`
- **Retrograde, direct.** Apparent motion backward, or forward, through the zodiac. Keep *direct motion* apart from a *direct calculation*.
  **nb** retrograd / direkte (bevegelse) · **es** retrógrado / directo (movimiento directo) · **fr** rétrograde / direct · **de** rückläufig / direktläufig · **zh** 逆行 / 顺行（℞） · **ja** 逆行／順行
  *Not:* de: direkt (Verwechslung mit direkter Berechnung)
- **Right ascension (RA).** Position along the celestial equator, counted eastward from 0° Aries in degrees or in hours.
  **nb** rektascensjon (RA) · **es** ascensión recta (AR) · **fr** ascension droite (AD) · **de** Rektaszension (RA) · **zh** 赤经（RA） · **ja** 赤経（RA）
  *Not:* fr: `RA`

## S

- **Secondary motion.** A planet's own motion forward through the zodiac, against the primary motion.
  **nb** sekundærbevegelsen *(proposed)* · **es** movimiento secundario *(proposed)* · **fr** mouvement secondaire *(proposed)* · **de** Sekundärbewegung *(proposed)* · **zh** 第二运动 *(proposed)* · **ja** 第二運動 *(proposed)*
- **Sexagesimal.** Counting in sixties: 60 seconds make a minute, 60 minutes a degree. Render the idea, not the calque "in sixty".
  **nb** sekstitallsystemet («regning med 60 som grunntall») · **es** base sesenta (de sesenta en sesenta) *(proposed)* · **fr** sexagésimal (« compter par soixante ») · **de** sexagesimal (Rechnen in Sechzigern) · **zh** 六十进制（以六十计数） · **ja** 六十進法（六十で数える）
  *Not:* nb: `i seksti` (calque)
- **Sidereal day.** One turn of the sky: 23h 56m 04s.
  **nb** stjernedøgn · **es** día sidéreo · **fr** jour sidéral · **de** Sterntag · **zh** 恒星日 · **ja** 恒星日
- **Sidereal time (ST).** Time kept by the stars rather than the Sun; the column heading of a table of houses.
  **nb** stjernetid (ST) · **es** tiempo sidéreo (TS) · **fr** temps sidéral (TS) · **de** Sternzeit · **zh** 恒星时（ST） · **ja** 恒星時（ST）
  *Not:* es: `hora sideral`, `tiempo sideral`, `ST`
- **Sign.** One of the twelve 30° divisions of the zodiac. Keep it apart from the arithmetic sign (±): *see* Change sign, Sign key.
  **nb** tegn (dyrekretstegn) · **es** signo · **fr** signe (du zodiaque) · **de** Zeichen (Tierkreiszeichen) · **zh** 星座（星座内度数） · **ja** サイン
  *Not:* nb: fortegn (that is the ± sign); de: Vorzeichen (das ist ±); zh: 宫（指星座时）; ja: 星座, `星座（サイン）`
- **Sign key.** The Takht key that enters and shows longitudes as sign and degree. Its caption stays *sign* in every language.
  **nb** sign-tasten (vis innenfor tegnet) · **es** tecla sign (sign activado) · **fr** touche sign · **de** Taste sign (Im Tierkreiszeichen anzeigen) · **zh** sign 键（开启 sign） · **ja** sign キー
- **Station, stationary.** Where a planet seems to stand still as it turns retrograde or direct.
  **nb** stasjon / stasjonær · **es** estación / hacer estación *(proposed)* · **fr** station (faire station) / stationnaire · **de** Stillstand / stationär · **zh** 留 · **ja** 留（留になる）
  *Not:* zh: `停滞`
- **Synodic month.** From one meeting of Sun and Moon to the next: 29.53 days.
  **nb** synodisk måned · **es** mes sinódico · **fr** mois synodique · **de** synodischer Monat · **zh** 朔望月 · **ja** 朔望月

## T

- **Table of houses.** A printed table of the Midheaven, Ascendant and cusps, entered by sidereal time (columns) and latitude (rows).
  **nb** hustabell · **es** tabla de casas · **fr** table des maisons · **de** Häusertabelle · **zh** 宫位表 · **ja** ハウス表（恒星時で表を引く）
  *Not:* fr: `tableau des maisons`
- **Takht.** Persian *takht*, the dust board on which reckoners worked; the name of the calculator. Capitalised as a title.
  **nb** Takhten (støvtavle) · **es** el takht (tabla de polvo) · **fr** Takht (table à poussière) · **de** das Takht (Staubbrett) · **zh** Takht 沙盘（尘板） · **ja** タフト（砂盤）
  *Not:* fr: takht as name; ja: Takht in running Japanese text
- **Thirds.** The sexagesimal unit below the second: a sixtieth of a second.
  **nb** tredjedeler · **es** terceros · **fr** tierces · **de** Terzen · **zh** 微 · **ja** 秒の下の単位（thirds）
- **Time and arc.** Two measures of one turning: 1h = 15°, 4m = 1°. ×15 turns time into arc, ÷15 arc into time. Arc is not *angle*: keep the two words apart.
  **nb** tid ↔ bue (×15, ÷15) · **es** tiempo ↔ arco (×15, ÷15) · **fr** temps ↔ arc (×15, ÷15) · **de** Zeit ↔ Bogen (×15, ÷15) · **zh** 时间 ↔ 角度（×15，÷15） · **ja** 時間 ↔ 角度（×15, ÷15）
  *Not:* de: Winkel (für arc); ja: 弧 (for the quantity)
- **Tropical month.** The Moon's return to the same longitude: 27.32 days.
  **nb** tropisk måned · **es** mes trópico · **fr** mois tropique · **de** tropischer Monat · **zh** 回归月 · **ja** 回帰月
  *Not:* es: `mes tropical`
- **Tropical year.** The Sun's return to the same equinox: 365.2422 days.
  **nb** tropisk år *(proposed)* · **es** año trópico *(proposed)* · **fr** année tropique *(proposed)* · **de** tropisches Jahr *(proposed)* · **zh** 回归年 *(proposed)* · **ja** 回帰年 *(proposed)*
